X-Lab

AI-assisted customer messaging for small shops

X-Lab helps small shops in Myanmar and Thailand answer customer messages on Facebook Messenger and LINE. A shop owner connects their Facebook Page to X-Lab. X-Lab drafts replies to customer questions, and the shop owner checks each draft in a private Telegram or LINE chat before it is sent.
