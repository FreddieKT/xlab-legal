Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

X-Lab ("we", "us", "our") provides a messaging service for shops. A shop ("the Shop") connects its Facebook Page or LINE account to X-Lab, and X-Lab drafts replies to the Shop's customers. This policy explains what information X-Lab handles, why, and how you can ask us to delete it. It applies to Shop owners and to customers who message a Shop that uses X-Lab.

1. Information we collect

From customers who message a Shop:

  • The messages you send to the Shop's Page or LINE account, including text, stickers and images.
  • The ID the platform gives you for that Shop (for Messenger, the Page-Scoped ID), so a reply reaches you.
  • Order details you choose to share, such as name, phone number and delivery address.

From Shop owners:

  • Your name and contact details, and the Pages or accounts you connect.
  • Access tokens that Meta, LINE or Telegram issue when you connect an account. We store them in access-restricted storage and use them only to send and receive the Shop's messages.
  • Your replies and decisions on drafts (approve, edit, reject).

2. How we use information

  • To receive customer messages and draft replies for the Shop.
  • To show each draft to the Shop owner and send only the replies the Shop approves.
  • To pass orders and questions to the Shop owner.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell or rent personal information, use it for advertising, or send marketing messages. We reply only to people who messaged the Shop first.

3. Service providers

  • Meta (Facebook Messenger), LINE and Telegram — to receive and send messages.
  • An AI model provider — to draft replies. Message text is sent to the provider to create the draft and is not used by us to train models.
  • Cloud hosting — to run X-Lab.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

4. How long we keep information

We keep a Shop's messages, drafts and order details while the Shop uses X-Lab. When a Shop stops using X-Lab, or when you ask us (see Data Deletion), we delete that information within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

5. Your choices

  • Ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Shop owners can disconnect a Page or account at any time in X-Lab or in their Facebook settings.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access tokens are kept in access-restricted storage on our servers, and only the Shop's own worker uses them.

7. Children

X-Lab is a service for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new date.
