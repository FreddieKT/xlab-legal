Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 12 October 2026

You can ask X-Lab to delete the information it holds about you.

What can be deleted

  • Your messages to a Shop that uses X-Lab, and the drafts and replies to them.
  • Order details you shared, such as name, phone number and address.
  • For Shop owners: your account, connected Pages and accounts, access tokens, and your Shop's message history.

How to ask

Method 1 — Email

Email contactxplorerlab@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Tell us the name of the Shop you messaged and the platform (Messenger, LINE or Telegram), so we can find your records.

Method 2 — Message the Shop

Send the Shop the message "Please delete my data." The Shop will pass the request to X-Lab.

Shop owners

Disconnect X-Lab in your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations), then email us to delete your Shop's data.

What happens next

We confirm your request by email or message and delete the data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law. Deleting data cannot be undone.
